Household Load Context

Why Prepaid Balance ≠ Actual Electricity Consumed

  • Prepaid wallet deductions often include daily fixed demand charges, electricity duty, arrear installments, or slab adjustments alongside unit (kWh) charges.
  • A fast recharge deduction speed alone never proves a smart meter is faulty.
  • We do not apply guessed tariff rates. Always check your official utility app (e.g., MyAPDCL) and kWh meter reading before filing a dispute.
